One Pan Dumpling Dinner

Ingredients

  • 1 can coconut milk
  • 2 tbsp Thai curry paste
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 2 tsp rice vinegar
  • Minced garlic
  • Frozen dumplings

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F
  2. Whisk coconut milk, curry paste, garlic, soy sauce, oyster sauce, sesame oil, and rice vinegar together in a pan
  3. Layer frozen dumplings into the sauce
  4. Cover with foil, bake 30 min
  5. Uncover, bake another 10-15 min
  6. Top with chili crisp, green onions, and sesame seeds

Creamy Veggie & Sausage Boursin Cheese Orzo

Ingredients

  • 2 cups orzo
  • 1 pack Hillshire Farm beef sausage, sliced
  • Chicken Broth to cover orzo completely (about 3-4 cups)
  • 1 pack Garlic & Herb Boursin Cheese
  • Sun-dried tomatoes
  • Red onion
  • Minced Garlic
  • Italian seasoning
  • Oregano
  • Garlic Powder
  • Garlic Salt

Instructions

  1. Combine orzo, sliced beef sausage, sun-dried tomatoes, red onion, minced garlic, seasonings, and Boursin cheese in a baking dish. Pour enough chicken broth to cover the orzo completely (about 3-4 cups).
  2. Cover with foil and cook for 30 minutes at 425 degrees.
  3. Stir together and enjoy! If the pasta has not soaked up all the broth, put back in oven uncovered for 5-10 minutes until creamy.

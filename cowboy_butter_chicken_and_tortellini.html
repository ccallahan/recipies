Cowboy Butter Chicken Tortellini

Ingredients

For the chicken:

  • 4 thin sliced chicken breast
  • Seasoning for chicken: garlic powder, pepper, paprika, cowboy butter seasoning
  • Oil for drizzling over chicken and cooking

For the sauce & pasta:

  • 1 tablespoon of drippings from cooking chicken
  • 3 tablespoons of butter
  • 1 tablespoon of minced garlic
  • 1 cup chicken broth
  • 1 ½ cups heavy cream
  • 2 teaspoons cowboy butter seasoning
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • Black pepper to taste
  • 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
  • 1 teaspoon lemon juice
  • 18-ounce package of refrigerated tortellini
  • 1 ½ cups shredded parmesan
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar or mozzarella cheese
  • Chives for topping

Instructions

  1. Drizzle a little oil on your chicken and season with the seasonings listed.
  2. Add a drizzle of oil to a skillet heated over medium/high heat and cook your chicken to internal temp of 165 degrees. I flipped my chicken once and it took mine about 5 minutes on each side. When your chicken is done remove from the skillet and place it on a plate, leaving about 1 tablespoon of your drippings in the pan.
  3. Leave 1 tablespoon of drippings in your pan and add butter to melt. Once melted add in your garlic and stir and cook for 1 minute.
  4. Add the broth, cream, seasonings and Dijon mustard and mix. Add the lemon juice and while you add it make sure to stir as you add it.
  5. Bring it to a low boil and add in your tortellini. Cook for 5-7 minutes until your tortellini is tender and the liquid has started to thicken (the cheese will thicken it up).
  6. Once your tortellini are done add in your cheeses and stir to melt.
  7. I like to add my tortellini to a bowl and slice my chicken and lay it over the top and top everything with chives!

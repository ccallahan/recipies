Chicken Pot Pie Orzo Skillet

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 ½ lb chicken breast
  • ½ teaspoon sea salt - divided
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper - divided
  • 1 onion - diced
  • 2 cloves garlic - minced
  • 3 carrots - cut into half moons
  • 3 stalks celery - diced
  • ½ cup white wine - or more broth
  • 1 teaspoon oregano
  • ½ teaspoon thyme
  • ½ teaspoon paprika
  • 1 tablespoon arrowroot starch - or flour
  • 1 cup orzo - dry, use gluten free if needed
  • 3 cups chicken broth - low sodium
  • ¼ cup coconut milk - or cream
  • ½ cup peas - frozen
  • ½ cup corn
  • Parmesan cheese - optional, for garnish

Instructions

  1. Heat olive oil and a skillet over medium heat and cook the chicken breast with salt and pepper. Once the chicken is done and transferred to a plate and set aside.
  2. To the same skillet, add the onions and garlic and sauté until fragrant, then add the carrots and celery and cook until tender.
  3. Deglaze the pan with white wine or broth.
  4. Add the spices, starch, and remaining salt and pepper over the vegetables and mix.
  5. Add the orzo to the skillet with the chicken broth and stir to prevent the orzo from sticking to the skillet. Cook until the orzo is al dente.
  6. Stirring the coconut milk, followed by the peas, corn and chicken breast.
  7. Serve into bowls and garnish with optional parmesan cheese and enjoy.

Bacon Egg and Cheese Hot Pockets

Ingredients

  • 6 Rice Paper Sheets
  • 12 Eggs
  • Bacon
  • Cheese

Instructions

  1. Dip rice paper sheets into egg wash and lay them on a cutting board.
  2. Lay scrambled eggs, cheese, and bacon on top and fold into pockets.
  3. Place in an air fryer at 375°F for 8-12 minutes depending on how you like the color.

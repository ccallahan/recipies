Strawberry Shortcake Cream Cheese Brownies

Ingredients

BROWNIES:

  • 1 box strawberry cake mix
  • 1/2 c butter, melted
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tablespoons strawberry jello

FROSTING:

  • 8 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1/4 c butter, softened
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • 1/2 c confectioners sugar
  • 1-2 tablespoons heavy cream

TOPPING:

  • 20 golden Oreos
  • Remaining packet of strawberry jello
  • 1/4 c melted butter

Instructions

Brownies:

  1. Mix together and combine well and spread batter into an 8x8 prepared baking dish. Bake at 350 for 25 minutes and let cool completely.

Frosting:

  1. Beat the cream cheese, butter and vanilla. Slowly add in the sugar and continue beating adding cream as needed until smooth and creamy.
  2. Spread evenly over the cooled brownies.

Topping:

  1. Add the Oreos to a food processor and pulse until chunky. Pour in the jello and butter and continue pulsing until crumbly.
  2. Sprinkle over the top of the frosting.

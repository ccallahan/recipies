Beef & Guinness Slow Cooker Stew

Ingredients

  • 700g to 1kg stewing beef or lamb, seared
  • 1 onion, chopped and browned
  • 3 carrots, chopped
  • A handful of fresh parsley
  • 1 can of Guinness
  • 300ml beef stock
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp dried thyme, optional
  • 1 tbsp cornflour mixed with 2 tbsp cold water

Method

  1. Sear the beef or lamb in a hot pan until beautifully browned.
  2. Add the meat to your slow cooker with the onion, carrots and parsley.
  3. Pour in the Guinness and beef stock, then season with salt, pepper and thyme.
  4. Pop the lid on and cook on low for 6 to 8 hours or high for 4 to 5 hours, until the meat is lovely and tender.
  5. Stir in the cornflour mixture towards the end and cook for another 15 to 20 minutes, until the gravy has thickened.

Mississippi Beef and Rice Skillet

Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 ½ cups long grain rice (rinsed)
  • 3 ½ cups beef broth
  • 1/4 cup pepperoncini pepper brine
  • 1 packet au jus gravy
  • 1 packet ranch seasoning
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • Black pepper to taste
  • 5 pepperoncini peppers sliced up (use more or less depending on spice preference)

Instructions

  1. In a hot skillet brown your beef until it isn't pink anymore and is cooked. Drain off any excess grease. Add your butter in to melt and once its melted add your rice in and let that toast a few minutes while you stir it.
  2. Add in your au jus, ranch pack, garlic powder, onion powder, pepper, broth, brine from the peppers, and sliced peppers. Stir it all together and bring to a boil.
  3. Once it comes to a boil reduce the heat to low and cover. Let it cook for 20 minutes to make sure your rice is done, I like to add extra sliced peppers to my bowl.

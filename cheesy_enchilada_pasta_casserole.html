Cheesy Enchilada Pasta Casserole

A rich, high-protein pasta bake packed with ground beef, chorizo, peppers, and a creamy blended queso sauce.

Ingredients

Meat & Vegetable Base

  • 32 oz 93% ground beef
  • 8 oz pork chorizo
  • 120 g red enchilada sauce
  • 14 oz (400g) can fire roasted tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp garlic purée
  • 1 tbsp chipotle peppers
  • 1 tbsp adobo sauce
  • 3 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 2 orange bell peppers
  • 2 red bell peppers
  • 1 onion

Spice Blend

  • 1 tbsp coarse salt
  • 1 tbsp ground paprika
  • 1 tbsp ground oregano
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1.5 tsp ground black pepper

Pasta

  • 672 g pasta, cooked to 50% (cook for half the time shown on the package)

Blended Queso Sauce

  • 800 g 2% cottage cheese
  • 100 g 1/3 fat cream cheese
  • 150 g extra sharp cheddar cheese
  • 30 g cheddar powder
  • 240 g red enchilada sauce
  • 300 ml milk
  • Salt & pepper to taste

Optional Garnish

  • Chopped cilantro

Instructions

  1. Prepare the Spice Blend: Mix together salt, paprika, oregano, cumin, and black pepper.
  2. Cook Meat & Vegetables: Combine ground beef, chorizo, spice blend, 120g enchilada sauce, fire-roasted tomatoes, garlic purée, chipotle peppers, adobo sauce, apple cider vinegar, chopped bell peppers, and onion.
    • Oven Method: Bake at 400°F for 20-25 minutes.
    • Slow Cooker Method: Cook on High for 2-3 hours OR Low for 3-4 hours.
  3. Prepare Pasta: Cook 672g of pasta for half of the cook time listed on the package instructions. Drain well.
  4. Make Queso Sauce: In a high-speed blender, combine cottage cheese, cream cheese, extra sharp cheddar, cheddar powder, 240g enchilada sauce, milk, and salt & pepper to taste. Blend until smooth.
  5. Combine & Heat: Combine the cooked meat mixture, parboiled pasta, and blended queso sauce. Cover and cook on High for 15-20 minutes until heated through and cheese is melted. Garnish with chopped cilantro if desired.

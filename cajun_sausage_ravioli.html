Ingredients

  • 1 lb sausage (breakfast or Italian)
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1/2 red bell pepper, diced
  • 1/2 green bell pepper, diced
  • 1/2 sweet onion, diced
  • 2 tbsp minced garlic
  • 1 tbsp Cajun seasoning (e.g., Fire & Smoke Society)
  • Black pepper to taste
  • 1 cup broth
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1 cup shredded Parmesan cheese
  • 25 oz bag ravioli, cooked according to package instructions and drained

Instructions

  1. Melt butter in a skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. Add diced red bell pepper, green bell pepper, and sweet onion. Sauté for a few minutes.
  3. Add sausage to the skillet. Brown and cook until fully done. Drain off any excess grease.
  4. Add minced garlic, Cajun seasoning, and black pepper. Stir well and cook for 1 minute.
  5. Pour in broth and heavy cream. Bring the mixture to a low boil.
  6. Add shredded Parmesan cheese, stirring until melted, then remove from heat.
  7. Fold in the cooked and drained ravioli. Let it simmer for a few minutes before serving.

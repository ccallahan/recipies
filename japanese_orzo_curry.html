Japanese Curry Orzo

Per serving (makes 13): 595 Calories | 40g Protein | 79g Carbs | 14g Fat

These store INCREDIBLY well frozen, just toss them in the microwave for ~5 minutes to reheat and it’s as good as fresh!

Ingredients

CHICKEN

  • 1350g (48oz) chicken breast
  • Salt, black pepper & garlic powder
  • Shichimi togarashi, optional

VEGGIES/AROMATICS

  • 30g (2 Tbsp) oil
  • 500g (~4 large) carrots, diced
  • 450g (3 medium) sweet onions, diced
  • 60g (4 Tbsp) tomato paste
  • 45g (3 Tbsp) ginger paste

ORZO & COOKING LIQUID

  • 896g (32oz) orzo
  • 700g (25oz) frozen diced potatoes
  • 30g (2 Tbsp) honey
  • 30g (2 Tbsp) soy sauce
  • 1440g (48oz) chicken bone broth
  • 960g (32oz) water

FINISHING INGREDIENTS

  • 270g (15 cubes) Golden Curry Japanese curry roux
  • 15g (1 Tbsp) toasted sesame oil
  • Green onions
  • Sesame seeds
  • Add 1-2 cups extra water at end as needed, to loosen

Instructions

  1. Season chicken with salt, pepper, garlic powder & optional shichimi togarashi. Cover with foil and bake at 375°F for 30 minutes. Dice once cooked.
  2. Heat oil in a 7-qt or larger pot. Add carrots and onions; cook until softened and browned. Add tomato paste and ginger; cook 2–3 minutes.
  3. Add orzo, honey, soy sauce, potatoes, water & bone broth. Bring to a boil, reduce to low and simmer 20–30 minutes until orzo is cooked, stirring frequently to prevent sticking.
  4. Chop curry cubes and stir in until melted. Add 1–2 cups water as needed to loosen (texture should be thick but creamy).
  5. Mix in chicken, green onions, sesame oil & sesame seeds. Divide into 13 servings, and enjoy!

Note: Japanese curry cubes will significantly thicken once added to the orzo - make sure to add extra water at the end incrementally to create proper texture. Amount needed will vary, I added ~1.5 cups of extra water to create the texture shown in the video!

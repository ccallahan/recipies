Sheet Pan Chicken Alfredo Bites

Ingredients

  • 2 jars Rao's Homemade alfredo sauce
  • 2 bags Kinder's Flavors frozen original chicken bites
  • shredded mozzarella cheese
  • fresh parsley (chopped)
  • shredded parmesan cheese

Instructions

  1. On a foil-lined baking sheet, bake frozen chicken bites at 425° for 25 minutes.
  2. On another foil-lined baking sheet, pour alfredo sauce evenly, followed by an even layer of shredded mozzarella cheese and parsley.
  3. Bake at 425° for 8-10 minutes, and broil on high for 2-3 minutes until cheese is melted and bubbly.
  4. Slide and pour alfredo pan over baked chicken bites, then top with a little shredded parmesan.

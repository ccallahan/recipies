Queso Steak Fajita Rice

Ingredients

  • Strip steak or flank steak (about 2 lbs)
  • 1 container/jar of your favorite queso
  • 2 bell peppers, sliced
  • 1 medium white onion, sliced
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 1 cup white rice
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 packet of taco seasoning
  • 1 (8oz.) can of tomato sauce
  • 2 cups chicken broth
  • Cilantro for garnish

Recipe

  1. Start by slicing your veggies and sauté in olive oil for about 5-7 min.
  2. After about 5 mins, throw in 1/4 cup of water, place a lid on your pan and place on medium heat to help the veggies break down. (If you like a firmer veggie, skip this step)
  3. Once your veggies are soft, toss in your rice and toast for about a minute
  4. Add in your seasoning and stir
  5. Once partially combined, add in your tomato sauce and chicken broth and stir
  6. Place the lid on your pan again and simmer for about 12-15 mins or until rice is fully cooked.
  7. In a separate pan, fry up your steak in a little olive oil to temperature preference.
  8. Once steak is cooked, place steak on the fajita rice mixture
  9. Top with warmed up queso, cilantro for garnish and enjoy!

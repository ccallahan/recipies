Cajun Sausage and Shrimp Rigatoni

Ingredients

  • Rigatoni
  • Andouille sausage
  • Shrimp
  • Olive oil
  • PoggioliEats Cajun seasoning
  • Roasted garlic butter
  • Red bell pepper
  • Green bell pepper
  • Shallot
  • Garlic
  • Heavy cream
  • Lemon
  • Parmesan
  • Fresh parsley

Instructions

  1. Cook the rigatoni in salted water until just shy of al dente. Reserve some pasta water before draining.
  2. Slice the andouille into rounds and sear in a large pan until browned and crispy. Remove and set aside.
  3. Toss the shrimp with olive oil and Cajun seasoning. Add olive oil to the same pan and sear the shrimp for 45–60 seconds. Flip and cook for another 30–60 seconds, until just cooked through. Remove and set aside.
  4. Add roasted garlic butter to the pan, followed by the diced red and green bell peppers. Cook for a couple minutes, then add the diced shallot and cook until softened.
  5. Add the minced garlic and cook until fragrant. Pour in the heavy cream, season with more Cajun seasoning, and add a squeeze of lemon juice. Let the sauce gently simmer and thicken.
  6. Add the rigatoni, shrimp, and andouille back to the pan and toss everything together. Reduce the heat to low and mix in plenty of freshly grated Parmesan. Add pasta water as needed until the sauce is smooth and creamy.
  7. Top with fresh parsley, Parmesan, and a final squeeze of lemon juice. Serve immediately.

Chipotle Barbacoa

Ingredients

  • 2lbs chuck roast
  • 1/2 can chipotle peppers in adobo sauce
  • 2 teaspoons dried oregano
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons cumin
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons salt
  • 3/4 teaspoon ground pepper
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 3 tablespoons avocado oil
  • 3 tablespoons water
  • 4 garlic cloves

Instructions

  1. Cube the chuck roast into even pieces. Get your pan hot with oil, then sear every side until you’ve got a deep, dark crust (tons of flavor).
  2. Toss the chipotle peppers in adobo sauce, oregano, cumin, salt, pepper, garlic, oil, and water into a blender. Blend until it’s silky smooth.
  3. Seared beef goes into the slow cooker first, sauce poured on top, bay leaves last.
  4. Low and slow for 3-4 hours, until the meat shreds apart with ease.

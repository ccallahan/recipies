Cajun Sausage Ravioli
-1 pound of sausage (breakfast or Italian)
-2 tbsp butter
-1/2 a red bell pepper diced
-1/2 a green bell pepper diced
-1/2 a sweet onion diced
-2 tbsp minced garlic
-1 tbsp Cajun seasoning Fire & Smoke Society
-black pepper to taste
-1 cup of broth
-1 cup heavy cream
-1 cup shredded Parmesan
-25 ounce bag ravioli cooked according to the package and drained
Melt your butter in a skillet, heated to medium high heat. Add in your peppers and onions and sauté for a few minutes. Add in your sausage and brown it and cook till it’s done. Drain off any excess grease. Add your minced garlic, Cajun seasoning and pepper mix it in good and let that cook for a minute. Add in your broth and heavy cream. Bring that up to a low boil and add in your Parmesan and stir that to melt and cut the heat. Fold in cooked and drained ravioli. I like to let mine simmer for a few minutes.

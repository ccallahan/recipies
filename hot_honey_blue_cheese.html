Hot Honey Bleu Cheese

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup mayonnaise
  • 1/4 cup sour cream
  • 3 tbsp evaporated milk or buttermilk
  • 5 oz crumbled bleu cheese
  • 2 tbsp hot honey
  • 2 tbsp Sriracha
  • 2 tsp spicy dry rub seasoning
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 2 tsp dried parsley
  • 1 tsp dried dill

Instructions

  1. Add all of the ingredients to a large mixing bowl.
  2. Whisk everything together until well combined—but make sure to leave those delicious bleu cheese crumbles nice and chunky!
  3. Store in a sealed container in the refrigerator for up to two weeks!
